
MBA Project Framing

Problem:
Travelers spend significant time comparing airfare, weather, destination costs, travel time, and activities across multiple websites.

Solution:
A personalized AI-assisted decision-support dashboard that consolidates those variables and ranks destinations based on the traveler's priorities.

AI / analytics components:
- normalization of heterogeneous variables
- weighted multi-criteria decision model
- personalized ranking
- explainable recommendations
- optional generative-AI explanation layer

Demo:
Use three traveler profiles to demonstrate that the same destinations rank differently when user preferences change.
